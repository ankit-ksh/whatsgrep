whatsgrep GitHub

Never miss the message that matters.

whatsgrep watches the WhatsApp chats you choose and alerts you the moment a message matches your rules.

Download

Windows· macOS (Apple silicon)· macOS (Intel)· Linux (x64)· Linux (ARM)

Rules in plain words

Pick the chats, the words that count and the words that don't. Test as you type.

Alerts where you are

On your computer, on your phone, or both. Every match is kept in a searchable history.

Quiet and private

Runs on your machine, starts at login, and only listens. It never sends a message.
